Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: April 2026

AB CARE AFH is committed to providing an accessible experience both in our physical home and on this website.

In the home

Our house in Everett has wheelchair-accessible entryways, grab bars in bathrooms, a walk-in shower, accessible bedrooms, and clear pathways. Tours are available by appointment so family members can see accessibility features firsthand.

On this website

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards. That means keyboard navigation, screen-reader-friendly structure, descriptive alt text on informative images, sufficient color contrast, respect for reduced-motion preferences, and labeled form fields.

Found a barrier?

If any part of this site or our home isn't working for you, please tell us. Email genetweledgeberiel2@gmail.com or call +1 (206) 834-5724 and we'll work with you to find a solution.
