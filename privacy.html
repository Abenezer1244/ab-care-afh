Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

AB CARE AFH respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit our contact form, we receive the name, email, phone number, and any message you choose to share. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers on this site.

How we use it

Form submissions are sent directly to our email (processed by FormSubmit.co) so that a caregiver or the owner can reply to you personally. We do not sell or share your information.

Data retention

Messages live in our email inbox for as long as we reasonably need them to respond. You can request deletion at any time by emailing us.

Third-party services

We use Google Fonts (served from fonts.googleapis.com), Google Maps (embedded on the contact page), and FormSubmit.co for form handling. These services have their own privacy policies.
